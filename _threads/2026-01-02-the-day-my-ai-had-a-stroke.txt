---TWEET---
🧠💥 I asked my AI about a diff viewer.

It responded with 3,000 lines of a Lua programming manual.

In English AND Chinese.

This is the story of the day my AI had a stroke. 🧵👇
---TWEET---
The setup: Normal debugging session. Context at 69% (688K/1M tokens). I asked a simple question about how the diff output would look.

The response: "Programming in Lua 第三版... 四本书... 1 – Introduction... 1 – 介绍..."

Page. After. Page. 😱
---TWEET---
The diagnosis: MaxTokensReachedException + training data leakage.

The model hit some internal limit, and instead of failing gracefully, it started dumping cached training data from its training set.

Pattern matching collapsed to "memorized state" 🤯
---TWEET---
Recovery was instant: Ctrl+C, screenshot for evidence, restart session.

No rehabilitation needed. No lasting damage.

Just me staring at my terminal wondering if the singularity is really ready when AI can't maintain a coherent conversation 😂
---TWEET---
Lessons learned:
✅ Backup your AI conversations
✅ High context = higher risk of weird failures  
✅ LLMs aren't thinking—they're pattern matching
✅ When patterns break, chaos ensues
