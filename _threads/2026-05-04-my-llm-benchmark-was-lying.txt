I spent a week building an LLM benchmark.

The first two runs gave completely different rankings. Neither was real.

Here are 6 ways my tooling lied to me. 🧵👇
---TWEET---
Lie 1: Piping prompts to `ollama run` merges them into one turn.
Lie 2: ANSI escape codes break code extraction silently.
Lie 3: `keep_alive` means only the first benchmark gets a cold load.

Fix: Ollama HTTP API + stop-per-benchmark.
---TWEET---
Lie 4: Disk fills silently mid-run (293 GB of models, zero free space, no error).
Lie 5: caxi said "no canvas" on 10/10 pages. grep showed 9/10 had canvas. Stale Chrome session.
Lie 6: Vision models rated every UI 3/5 regardless of quality.

Fix: Playwright + pairwise Opus.
---TWEET---
After fixing all 6 lies, the leaderboard flipped:

• gemma4 went from "probably mid" to #1 (visual polish carried it)
• qwen3-coder went from "obvious winner" to #5 (games-only specialist)

Your tools lie. Test the tests.
