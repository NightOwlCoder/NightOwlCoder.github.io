Why the deck reads bland

Measured — the deck's own section headings:

residents on this machine · screens · commands · entry points
skills · task cards · the nightly train · tools the model holds · the wire

Every one names a part of the system. None names a thing that happens to a person. The charters do the opposite, and 9 of 12 already have the shape:

"Come to me when words ship as him…"                — 🎙️ Cyrano
"Message Polaris to find out whether something is stuck…" — ⭐ Polaris
"…before adding a sweep category."                     — 📬 Carteiro

A feature answers "what is it". A moment answers "when would I want this". The deck is an inventory and inventories cannot tease, because nothing in an inventory is addressed to the reader.

The rotation

One teaser per day, same for every visitor that day, chosen without a server:

const day = Math.floor(Date.now() / 864e5);      // days since epoch, UTC
const today = moments[day % moments.length];     // 12 moments → a 12-day cycle
PropertyWhy it matters
same for everyone Two people who talk about it saw the same thing. Random per-visit breaks that.
no server, no storage A static page can ship it. No cookie, no consent banner, nothing to be wrong about.
UTC, not local Local midnight means the "daily" teaser changes while a group is looking at it.
deterministic Day 6 is always moment 6 — so a link can name a day and be honest.

A 12-day cycle is a consequence, not a choice. Twelve seats, so twelve moments to start; the cycle lengthens as moments are added, and no code changes. Growth is copy, not engineering.

today's row is marked · every row is a question, never a feature name

The list is the archive and the proof at once: twelve questions and not one product noun. If a row cannot be written as something the visitor has felt, that seat has no teaser yet — and that is information, not a gap to fill with a feature.

Rejected, with reasons

ShapeWhy not
feature grid, 12 tiles That is the deck, and the deck was read as bland. A grid asks the visitor to shop; a moment asks them to recognise themselves.
random per visit Two people cannot discuss what they saw. Daily is shared; random is private.
carousel that auto-advances It removes the one thing a teaser has — a single thing to look at. And nobody has ever finished one.
screenshot of the product A screenshot of a terminal is a picture of work. The moment is the relief, and relief does not photograph.
email capture before the archive The way out must be free. A teaser that charges for the next one is an ad, and the visitor knows the difference.
