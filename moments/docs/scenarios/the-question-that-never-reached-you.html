part one

The story

One agent asks you everything. Two agents ask you twice as much. The reason "more agents" has meant "more interruptions" is that every one of them was wired straight to you. Here they are wired to each other, in a fixed order, and the order is the product: a question is answered at the lowest level that can answer it, and the level above hears about it only if it could not.

part two

How it is sold

One moment a day. This is moment 11 of 12; the format and the others are in the teaser format study.

The four parts of this teaser

PartThis scenarioWhy
the question How many questions did your agents ask each other today, so they wouldn't have to ask you? It reframes the fear ("more agents, more noise") as a count the reader can check. The number is the product.
one beat of proof A question climbs one level at a time and stops at the first level that may answer it. A structural rule, not a promise of quiet. The reader can imagine the pyramid; that is the point.
who does it 🌶️ Pepper · chief of staff She is the top of the pyramid below you. Her charter: receives asks, delegates to the owner, keeps the ledger, follows up. The residue reaches you through her.
the way out see all 12 → Always visible.

Rejected wording: "multi-agent orchestration", "hierarchical agents", "reduces interruptions by 80%". The percentage is unmeasurable in general and would be a claim. What is sold is the order, and that the order is looked up, not trusted.

What keeps it honest

The pyramid is doctrine written into code and specs: SPEC_PORTO §4, "asks flow UP; each level absorbs maximally and escalates only what it cannot answer". The mechanisms per rung are real: ask_caller pauses the asking agent's task until its dispatcher answers; a run's supervisor is woken by engine events, never by a person; the operator is reached only through ask_human (a fire alarm, never a status feed) or through Pepper. Pepper's ledger on this machine has 14 asks, 7 closed, each with the ask in the operator's words and the owner it went to. The "seven questions, none over 4 minutes" line is the shape of an answer the ledger could give; the count is not composed today.

part three

How it is built

Measured on main. Every rung exists with its own channel and its own guarantee. The gap is the count: the climb is recorded in pieces, not as one ledger the person can read.

drawn against: SPEC_PORTO §4–§6 · commands/ask_caller.rs · commands/answer_task.rs · engine/mod.rs:1682–1767 · commands/escalate_to_human.rs · commands/ask_human.rs · notify_operator.rs · Pepper's charter and asks.md

The rungs

RungAbsorbsEscalates viaGuarantee
worker (marujo) Everything its brief and worktree answer. ask_caller — one tight question; pauses its task until the answer arrives as the tool result. It cannot proceed on a guess: the task is Blocked until answer_task.
run lead (sede) Plan and feature questions, worker disagreements, salvage calls. Engine wake to its supervisor on finished/failed/blocked/stall/STUCK. Continue-loop: three nudges without progress → STUCK → supervisor woken once for the episode. "Budget, not faith."
supervisor (porto / Marco) Cross-run judgment, grants, steer-or-kill. ask_human for product, credentials, taste — or an ask to Pepper. While a worker's escalation to the human is pending, the run cannot be abandoned: end_feature is blocked until the operator answers.
chief of staff (Pepper) Delegation to the owning seat, follow-up, the ledger. The phone, inside the push window; a gate, at any hour. Every ask has a durable key and the operator's own words; the state column is the crew's, the ask column is yours.
you Product calls, credentials, permissions, taste. — Paged by fire alarm, never by status feed.

Five records, five stores. Everything in "seven questions, four by the lead, two by Marco, one by you" is present somewhere; nothing joins it. That join is the whole gap.

What does not exist yet

GapWhat it takes
The count, per project per day A projection over the five stores keyed by run and day: asks raised, at which rung answered, longest wait. The ask key from scenario 01 travelling outbound gives every question a project to belong to.
"[ Ask me tonight ]" A deferral button that re-raises the gate inside the next push window. The gate already has a deadline; a "later" resolution that re-queues is small.
A product question reaching Pepper from a run Today a run's supervisor calls ask_human directly. Routing product questions through Pepper (so they land in her ledger with the ask in your words) is the designed shape and not the wired one.

Rejected, with reasons

ShapeWhy not
Every agent may ask the human Two agents would ask twice as much. Only the top of the pyramid pages; everyone else asks up one level.
Skip levels when it's urgent Urgency is what a fire alarm is for, and a gate reaches the phone at any hour. Questions are not urgent; they are owned.
Answer on the agent's behalf when the human is slow A pending human escalation blocks abandonment by design. Slow is allowed; guessing is not.
A daily "questions asked" email Status never pages. The count is looked up when you want it, in chat.
