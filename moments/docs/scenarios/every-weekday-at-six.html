The story

"Recurring" is where assistants go to die. A scheduled prompt in a chat product lives in a tab; close the tab, change the model, restart the machine, and it is gone without a word. Here a schedule is a message delivered to a named agent by a clock the engine owns. The agent reads its own standing notes, does the job, and the result lands where results land.

it ran. The answer names the fire, the delivery, and where it went, not "should have"

Three things are different from a scheduled prompt. The target is a person on the crew, addressed by name, so the job follows her across sessions. The clock is the engine's: it is written to disk and comes back after a restart. And silence is not an outcome: a job that could not fire records the reason, and "did it run?" gets a fact, not a guess.

Rejected wording: "automations", "workflows", "set it and forget it". The last one is the lie the wound came from. What is sold is that you can ask whether it ran and get an answer with a timestamp.

What keeps it honest

Every claim is read from this machine. Job 15 in the live cron table is exactly the story's job: 0 6 * * 1-5, delivered to Bede, prompt "Your 06:00 morning open. Read career/2026/evergreen/scheduled-runs.md and follow the 06:00 section." Her standing notes, not a prompt written into the job. Job 28 is the story's expired one-off: expired: one-shot missed its scheduled fire at 2026-08-25T09:00, recorded, not silent. The 08:00 digest is the push window in notify_operator.rs: [08:00, 23:30), outside folds into one morning digest. Bede does not ship, so the story's Bede is a stand-in for whichever seat a stranger gives a morning routine to; the mechanism is identical for any name.

How it is built

Measured on main and on this machine's live job table. The clock, the name targeting, the durability and the failure reasons all exist. The gaps are in the conversation around them: Pepper setting a job up, and the "did it run?" answer as a sentence.

The clock

ClaimMechanismWhere
"addressed to her by name, so the job follows her" target_name: the resident name is resolved to the live session at each fire via lineage, so a resident that continued into a new session (/snt) still receives it. The alternative, target_session, pins a numeric id and goes stale. cron_create.rs:35
"survives restarts" Jobs are durable across daemon restarts; the table on this machine has ids 1–30 spanning weeks and several daemon rebuilds. cron_create.rs (tool description) · live table
"when it can't fire it says why" Expired jobs keep an expired_reason: one-shot done, count or deadline reached, dead target, or a missed fire. Job 28 on this machine reads expired: one-shot missed its scheduled fire at 2026-08-25T09:00:00. cron_list.rs:88 · live table
"first fire is tomorrow" next_fire is computed and shown per active job; end_after takes a fire count or a datetime; once for one-shots. cron_list.rs:37 · cron_create.rs
"the summary goes to your phone at 08:00" Push window [08:00, 23:30) half-open; outside-window items fold into one morning digest. One batched buzz per sweep, items deduped by caller-stable key, calendar-aware hold to meeting end (cap 2h). notify_operator.rs:14–34

The other clock: the nightly train

Recurring work the system does for itself rides a second scheduler, one platform job at 03:00 (dev.ilha.nightly on macOS) that runs every enabled job. Read live while writing this:

[ON ] checkpoint_prune          remove orphan + stale checkpoints
[ON ] checkpoint_turncap        cap each shadow repo at its last 5 commits
[ON ] empty_session_prune       delete empty session files older than 7 days
[ON ] memory_consolidate        Layer-1 memory: scan recent sessions, apply updates
[ON ] safety_consolidate        triage classifier disagreements, self-heal ~/.ilha/safety
[ON ] merged_feature_cleanup    sweep shipped features once their PR merges
[ON ] resident_transcript_sync  version baptised residents' transcripts with their identity
[OFF] worktree_gc               reap dead worktrees — dry-run unless confirmed
[ON ] ci_self_heal              run the suite on main, route each failure to a candidate

The last one is the shape the whole product has: the nightly runs the test suite and delivers each failure to the agent whose commit is a candidate, never to a person, and its own doc says "attribution names a candidate, never a culprit"; a run whose failures cannot be recovered is reported and never routed. Recurring work that wakes the right agent instead of paging you is the same idea as Bede's morning open, pointed inward.

Rejected, with reasons

ShapeWhy not
Target a session id Ids go stale when a resident continues into a new session. The name walks to the live tip; that is what makes "follows her" true.
Fire at 06:00 and push at 06:00 Status never wakes anyone. The job runs at six; the person hears at eight. The story asks and the answer keeps the window.
Silently skip a missed fire A missed one-shot is recorded with its reason. The Thursday question gets an answer because nothing was skipped quietly.
Put the routine in the prompt The job says "read your standing notes and follow the 06:00 section". The routine lives in a file the agent owns and can change; the schedule is just the knock.
