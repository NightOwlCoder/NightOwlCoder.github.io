Scenarios

One file per moment. Each reads top-down the way it will be shipped: the teaser, the story, how it is sold, how it is built. The teaser format and the twelve-moment rotation are in the teaser format study.

These files are the source of the public teaser page. scripts/teaser-site.py renders them: bare, into site/ for the LAN preview; --public runs the leak gate and writes site-public/; --deploy mirrors that into the blog checkout at moments/, which GitHub Pages serves at nightowlcoder.kovadj.dev/moments/. The blog push is the operator's.

Twelve of twelve, one per day of the cycle. Every card is signed by a seat that ships; the six seats born on Sergio's machine (voice, working record, delivery channels, screens, consent code, interface) appear inside stories as what a crew grows into, never as the signature. Ten are built today with a named gap; 12 is design and the card carries the label.

The shape every scenario follows

SectionWhat goes thereRule
the teaser The card as a visitor sees it, alone at the top. Question · one beat of proof · who · the way out. The question must be one the visitor already asks. The beat is a thing that happened, never a capability.
part one · the story The moment as a conversation, in the person's words. Two panes when the moment has a before/after. No identifiers in anything a person reads. Outcome first; detail only if asked.
part two · how it is sold The four parts of this teaser and why in that order; rejected wording; what keeps it honest. A teaser may run ahead of the release like a film teaser does, but every line points at something scheduled, never at the machine on one desk.
part three · how it is built What exists, with file:line; what does not, with what it takes; rejected shapes with reasons; an evidence-typed checklist; questions named with an owner. Measured on main. Nothing stated from memory. A gap is written as a gap.
