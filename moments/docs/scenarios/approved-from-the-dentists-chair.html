The story

Autonomy has a bad trade at its center: either the agent asks you everything and you become its babysitter, or it asks nothing and you find out later. The way out is to ask less, ask well, and ask wherever you are. On this machine that trade has been made 2,398 times from a phone, and 344 of those answers were No.

How it is sold

One moment a day. This is moment 7 of 12; the format and the others are in the teaser format study.

The four parts of this teaser

PartThis scenarioWhy
the question Ever come back to find your agent waiting for a Yes since 10am? The specific frustration of every "approve each step" tool: the work stopped the moment you looked away.
one beat of proof The command, the reason it was stopped, four buttons. Everything else keeps running. The beat is the shape of the question, not the existence of a phone app. Reason and justification side by side is what makes a two-second decision honest.
who does it 🌶️ Pepper · chief of staff Away mode is the operator handing Pepper the desk. The phone gate is engine machinery; the summary when you return is hers.
the way out see all 12 → Always visible.

Rejected wording: "human in the loop", "approval workflow", "full autonomy". The first two describe the babysitter; the third is the thing people rightly fear. What is sold is that the questions are few, well-formed, and reach you where you are, and that No is a normal answer with a normal consequence.

What keeps it honest

The numbers are this machine's: 2,398 rows in the phone-consent ledger, 1,993 approved, 344 denied, 23 approved with teach, 19 expired, 6 timed out. The four buttons are the ones the engine sends. The force-push having no teach button is real: teach is offered only for classifier-safe shapes, never for the hard-safety lines. "Two out of 31" is illustrative; the ratio on this machine over its life is 2,398 phone questions against hundreds of thousands of tool calls. The window rule applies: a gate is a fire alarm and reaches the phone at any hour; the 16:10 summary is status and would have folded into a digest had you not asked.

How it is built

Measured on main and on this machine's ledger. This is one of the oldest and most exercised paths in the product; the gaps are cosmetic.

drawn against: engine/sandbox_escalation.rs · engine/sandbox.rs · tools/consent/ · ask_human_requests (sessions.db) · commands/allow_list.rs · commands/ask_human.rs · commands/retire_buttons.rs · notify_operator.rs

The ladder

TierWhat happensWhere
0 — safe Read-only tools and commands the classifier reads as safe inside the run's own worktree run silently. The 29 of 31. tools/consent/classification.rs · engine/chat.rs:531 containment
2 — self-correct First refusal of a shape in a session is returned to the agent as a synthetic refusal. Most rejections are typos or a wrong path; the agent fixes itself and no human is involved. sandbox_escalation.rs:12
3 — a human The agent retried the same shape. Presence decides the route: Local renders the gate in the active client; Away sends it to Telegram with [Approve once] [Deny] [📚 Teach this WT] [🌍 Teach globally]. sandbox_escalation.rs:15–19
red list Disk-destruction shapes (rm -rf /, of=/dev/…, mkfs, shred) skip tier 2 and go straight to a human on the first attempt. Never teachable. engine/sandbox.rs:130 skip_tier2

Twenty-three columns in all; the ones above are the story. bubbled and ladder_state record the climb; pushover_* record the emergency channel, which is reserved for consent and never used for status. retire_buttons repairs prompts whose buttons outlived their answer (a daemon gap), stamping the stored verdict on each; idempotent.

Teach

ClaimMechanismWhere
"has run twice since without asking" A teach tap writes an allow-list row scoped to the worktree (or globally). Matching commands auto-approve silently without the classifier. 22 rows on this machine, all worktree-scoped. allow_list.rs:47 · sandbox_escalation.rs:354
"the force-push has no teach button" remember_available on the gate event is false for classifier-unsafe commands and for MCP tools; clients offer the affordance only when it is true. events.rs:335
"took the No and found another way" A denial is a normal tool result the agent reads; the run continues. Layer 1 rules push to a feature branch; main is never a push target for an agent. tools/consent/manager.rs apply_response · Layer 1 push rules

What does not exist yet

GapWhat it takes
"Two questions out of 31 commands" Per-run counts of silent vs gated calls are derivable from telemetry and the ledger but not composed as one sentence today. A client-side join, engine facts.
Pepper's return summary The ledger holds every answer; the "what happened while I was out" projection over replied_at since /away began is not a surface yet. Same shape as scenario 01's receipt.
"/away dentist, back by 4" — the time /away <msg> carries the message; a return time is not parsed. A deadline could shorten the default 24h on gates raised during the window.

Rejected, with reasons

ShapeWhy not
Ask about everything Tier 2 exists so the agent fixes its own typos. A human sees a shape only after the agent insisted on it.
Ask about nothing when away /away implies yolo for regular consent, not for the classifier. The red list and hard-safety lines are non-bypassable by any mode.
Teach the force-push Teach is for shapes that are safe to remember. Force-push to a shared branch is refused at tier 2 and escalated at tier 3 with no memory offered, by design.
A dashboard of pending approvals Nothing pends. A gate is delivered where you are, with a 24h deadline; the rest of the fleet does not wait behind it.
